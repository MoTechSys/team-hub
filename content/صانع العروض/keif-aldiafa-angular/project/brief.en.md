# Keif Al-Diafa Management System — a digital replacement for the paper ledger

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A hospitality company that staffs events with hosts ran bookings, equipment and receivables in a paper notebook: hosts double-booked, supplies running short when events overlapped, quotes and invoices written by hand.
**Solution:** I built a fully Arabic progressive web app in Angular 22 that runs with no server and works offline: a 4-step booking wizard, minute-level host conflict prevention, inventory shortage detection before it happens, automatic quote and invoice generation, a receivables ledger per host and client, debt aging with payment reminders, and equipment check-out/return matching with damage and loss — behind atomic permissions and custom roles.
**Results in numbers:** 232 green tests, 60 atomic permissions, 12 feature modules, 12 documented architecture decisions; money computed in integer halalas. Live user count: TBD.
**Who it helps:** Small service businesses that book people, hand out equipment and track receivables — with zero server cost.
