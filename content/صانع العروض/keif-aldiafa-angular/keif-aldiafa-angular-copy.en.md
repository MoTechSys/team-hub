## LinkedIn (≤120 words)
**A digital replacement for the hospitality paper ledger**

A hospitality company that staffs events with hosts ran bookings, equipment and receivables in a paper notebook: hosts double-booked, supplies short when events overlapped, quotes and invoices written by hand.

I built a fully Arabic progressive web app in Angular 22 that runs with no server and works offline: a 4-step booking wizard, minute-level host conflict prevention, inventory shortage detection before it happens, automatic quotes and invoices, a receivables ledger per host and client, debt aging, and equipment check-out/return matching.

232 green tests, 60 atomic permissions, money in integer halalas.

Let's turn your paper ledger into an app on your phone — DM or email.

That's what the paper notebook was doing at a Jeddah hospitality company — so I built a fully Arabic app that runs on a phone with no server and no internet: a 4-step booking wizard, minute-level host conflict prevention, inventory shortage detection before the event, one-tap quote and invoice, an "owed to / owed by" ledger per host and client, and equipment check-out/return matching with damage and loss.
