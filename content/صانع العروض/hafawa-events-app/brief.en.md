# Hafawa Events — Progressive Web App (PWA)

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A Riyadh hospitality company's customers want to request a quote from their phone in one tap, not browse a heavy site; the brand's website (hafawa-riyadh-events) is built for desktop browsing and search, not for the home screen.
**Solution:** A single-screen, vertical progressive web app in plain HTML/CSS/JS with no framework: full-screen hero, 6 services, a 6-item works gallery with lightbox, a 4-step "how we work", 4 FAQs, 12 covered districts, and a quote request via WhatsApp or call. Installable (manifest + Service Worker), works offline with a dedicated offline page, and serves responsive AVIF/WebP images.
**Results in numbers:** FCP 324 ms on a local mobile run · 27 requests / 562 KB per page load · HTML+CSS+JS 28.4 KB gzipped · 22 files in the offline cache · smallest font 12px · 0 console errors (sources in tech.md).
**Who it helps:** Hospitality and events business owners who want an "app" without an app store or subscription, and anyone who needs a booking page that lives on the home screen.
