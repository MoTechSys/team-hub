# Keif Hospitality — Arabic coffee service website for southern Saudi Arabia

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A hospitality provider in Abha needs a website that passes Google Ads business verification (privacy policy and terms), ranks in the seven southern service areas, and turns a visitor into a WhatsApp quote request — without inventing numbers or testimonials the client has not confirmed yet.
**Solution:** A statically exported Next.js 16 site (20 HTML pages) in full-RTL Arabic: a 9-section home page, 6 detailed services with a comparison table, 3 packages, 7 city pages generated from one data source, 8 FAQs feeding FAQPage schema, and a works gallery with 12 placeholders reserved for the client's real photos. Every unconfirmed fact is flagged `unverified` and is not rendered.
**Results in numbers:** tsc 0 errors · eslint 0 warnings · next build 21/21 pages · FCP 184 ms locally · CLS 0 · 59 requests / 1.55 MB (zero external requests) · 8 JSON-LD types · 5 commits in one day (sources in tech.md).
**Who it helps:** Regional event-service providers who want a site that passes Google Ads verification and runs on any static host with no server.
