# An SEO diagnostic pack that corrects its own conclusions by measurement

**Problem:** A 49-page "service × city" local-services site was accused, in a first-pass analysis, of duplicate content and broken code-generated URLs, with 301 redirects and page consolidation proposed — before anything was measured.
**Solution:** A full crawl of all 49 pages (26 fields each), a 49×49 similarity matrix with city names neutralised and two sanity controls, a source-level investigation of the 404s inside React's streaming markup, a mobile audit, and a Google-policy review quoting official text only — then a README that opens with four corrections of the earlier claims and keeps the withdrawn recommendation in a "rejected" folder with its reason.
