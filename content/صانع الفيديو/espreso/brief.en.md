# Espresso Coffee — A Luxury Site for a Local Coffee Roaster

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A specialty coffee shop with three branches in Al Hudaydah had only an Instagram account online — no menu, no branches, no story, and no fixed way for a phone visitor to get in touch.
**Solution:** A framework-free multi-page static site built with Vite: 6 pages (home, menu, ambience, story, branches, contact) in Arabic and English from one dictionary, a gallery of the account's real photos with a lightbox, a 15-drink menu in four categories, an installable PWA, and an image pipeline that emits AVIF/WebP at graded sizes.
**Results in numbers:** 6 pages × 2 languages, 15 drinks, 18 gallery photos, 139 AVIF + 139 WebP generated, 24 self-hosted font files, 69.5 KB JS and 62 KB CSS before compression, 0 console errors on a local run.
**Who it helps:** Café and small food-business owners who want a luxury site that runs on any static host with no server.
