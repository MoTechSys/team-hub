# Sayed Al-Diafa — event & hospitality rental website

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A Riyadh event contractor needs an Arabic site that runs on any static host — no Node, no build — and takes a mobile visitor straight to a WhatsApp quote request.
**Solution:** 15 pages of plain HTML/CSS/JS, fully RTL, mobile-first: home, services, packages, booking, gallery, coverage and 9 service pages. A booking form that becomes a pre-filled WhatsApp message, JSON-LD structured data, Open Graph and canonical on every page, WebP images with JPEG fallback.
**Results in numbers:** 15 pages · 0 dependencies · HTML+CSS ≈ 280 KB · 30 WhatsApp deep links · 56/56 images with alt text · 13 cities covered (sources in tech.md).
**Who it's for:** Small service businesses that want a fast site they can upload as-is and sell through WhatsApp.
