# Hesabati — post copy (EN)

## LinkedIn (≤120 words)
Running two or three shops on separate books and never seeing balances, debts, and payroll on one screen?

Hesabati is an Arabic-first finance system that puts all your businesses under one login: receipt and payment vouchers, journal entries, bank accounts and cash boxes, customers and suppliers, warehouses and purchasing, payroll, exchange rates, roles and permissions, and a real-time dashboard.

Built with Angular 21 and Tailwind on a Hono backend with PostgreSQL; one Docker command to run.

Measured from a live run: 50 screens, 84 tables, 158 passing backend tests.
