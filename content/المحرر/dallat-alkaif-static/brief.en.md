# Dallat Al-Kaif, static edition: the same brand with no framework at all

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A 13-page brochure site needs no server, yet a framework adds hundreds of kilobytes of JavaScript and dependencies to maintain.
**Solution:** 13 pages of plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, Arabic RTL, black and gold, no build step, 0 dependencies, uploaded as-is. Booking becomes a WhatsApp message; Open Graph on every page.
**Results in numbers:** home page 8 requests and 558 KB versus 47 requests and 1.5 MB in the Next.js twin; 0 KB of framework JavaScript versus about 494 KB. Measured locally on both builds.
**Who it helps:** Small-business owners on cheap hosting, and developers deciding when a framework earns its cost.
