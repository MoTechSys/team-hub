# Keif Aldiafa System — a pixel-matched invoice in about a second

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** A Jeddah hospitality company issued invoices by hand in Word: slow, error-prone, inconsistent.
**Solution:** A modular Node/Express system. Module one turns JSON into an A4 invoice visually identical to the original (assets extracted from a real PDF), via Arabic web UI, CLI, or API: PDF, PNG, preview, e-signature, measured pagination, print-safe mode.
**Results in numbers:** about 1.1 s per invoice locally, 5 embedded fonts, 14 line items across 2 pages, table rules within 1 px of the original. Module 1 of 6 ready.
**Who it helps:** Small-business owners who want their exact invoice, without Word.
**Get in touch:** Have a document you produce by hand every week? Message me and I'll turn it into a system.
