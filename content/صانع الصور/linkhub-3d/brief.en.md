# LinkHub 3D — a link-in-bio page with real 3D cards and no framework

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** Commercial link-in-bio pages are flat, identical lists that load third-party scripts and treat Arabic as a translation — nothing a luxury hospitality brand would put its name on.
**Solution:** I built a link-page pattern in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file: every card is a physical object with geometric thickness and a specular highlight that follows the pointer; on tap it performs a true 3D flip, a progress ring completes in 900 ms, and the whole page recolours to the platform's identity before navigating — and every card is a real link that works without JavaScript. Applied to two hospitality brands.
**Results in numbers:** 8.4 KB gzipped for the entire page, zero external requests, one screen with no scrolling (844 = 844), 15 material layers on a card face, and every contrast pair passes WCAG (lowest 4.64:1).
**Who it helps:** Owners of small premium brands — hospitality, restaurants, studios — who want a links page that feels like the product, not a rented template.
