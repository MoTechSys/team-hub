Sijil—a digital debt ledger a shop worker can use without reading.

In Yemeni shops, credit is written on paper and forgotten, the worker may not read or write, and there is no internet.

I built an offline-first Android app in Flutter: a debt entry in four steps (customer photo → took/paid → amount by banknotes → spoken confirmation), an eight-second undo, overdue aging at 30/60/90/180 days, PDF statements and receipts, and a separate ledger per currency.

## Instagram (≤150 words)
Credit on paper gets forgotten. Sijil keeps it.

An offline-first digital debt ledger for Yemeni shops, built for the owner and the worker alike—even one who cannot read: customer photo, a "took from me" or "paid me" button, the amount by pictured banknotes, then the app reads the entry aloud before saving. Made a mistake? Undo within eight seconds.

Overdue customers are aged at 30/60/90/180 days, with PDF statements and receipts, and a separate ledger per currency: Yemeni rial, Saudi riyal, US dollar.

Version 0.2.0 · 93 passing tests · 30-day offline trial.
