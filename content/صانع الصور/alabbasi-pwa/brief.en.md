# Alabbasi — an offline-first company PWA with zero dependencies

**Segment:** Companies
**Problem:** Company landing pages ship heavy frameworks and hundreds of dependencies, break without a connection, and treat Arabic as a second-class left-to-right language.
**Solution:** I built a company profile for a digital-services firm in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: fully RTL Arabic with Eastern Arabic numerals, offline via a Service Worker with three caching strategies, installable on mobile as a standalone app (manifest with three icons and three shortcuts), 18 inline SVG icons instead of emoji, and seven sections rendered from a single data source.
**Results in numbers:** 0 dependencies and no build step, ~18.6 KB of text assets gzipped, 7 files precached, the home page reloads offline straight from the Service Worker, and 0 console errors.
**Who it helps:** Company and organisation owners who want a fast digital presence that opens like an app and survives weak networks, without paying the complexity tax.
**Contact:** Does your company site need a strong connection just to show up? DM me and I'll build you one that works offline.
