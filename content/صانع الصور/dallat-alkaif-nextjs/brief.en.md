# Dallat Al-Kaif — a luxury hospitality site that tells only the truth

**Segment:** Small business
**Problem:** Hospitality websites promise "the whole Kingdom," show numbers and partner logos nobody owns, and are built with tools that break right-to-left Arabic.
**Solution:** I built a fully RTL Arabic site in Next.js 16 and React 19 with static export: 16 pages including one per service, 13 cities in exactly five southern regions, a single source of truth for every constant, self-hosted fonts, and content rules that block any out-of-scope city, invented statistic or numeric price.
**Result in numbers:** 16 static pages build successfully, type check and lint are clean, all four quality gates report zero violations, 48 px touch targets per WCAG 2.2, and 12 self-hosted font files at 452 KB.
**Who it's for:** Hospitality and events companies in southern Saudi Arabia who want a luxury site that sells no promises and sends visitors straight to WhatsApp.
