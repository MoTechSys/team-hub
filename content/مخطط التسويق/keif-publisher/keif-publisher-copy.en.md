# Publishing copy — keif-publisher

## LinkedIn (≈130 words)
Automated publishing is convenient — until a wrong post goes out on a real account: a caption that breaks brand language, an unintended price, or a post nobody reviewed.

keif-publisher starts from the safe default: kill switch on, dry-run always. A Python pipeline generates the caption, converts media to 9:16, and checks the text against 14 brand rules — then writes it as "pending review" only. A three-layer approval gate in code: the generator cannot write "approved", the dashboard refuses anything with a brand error, and live publishing refuses while the switch is on.

8 publishers with honestly declared capabilities, a scheduler with three attempts and a full log, a FastAPI + Jinja2 dashboard with no React — and 210 passing tests.
