# Publishing copy — Keif Aldiafa Ads Platform

## LinkedIn (≈130 words)
A Google Ads account whose budgets change, whose campaigns get switched on, whose keywords disappear — and nobody knows who did it or when.

Keif Aldiafa Ads Platform starts with the layer everyone is missing: an always-on connection. A Cloudflare Worker (Hono + D1) refreshes the access token automatically and caches it so Google is not hit on every request, plus 13 API endpoints led by a free-form GAQL query that opens the whole account to any agent or script, a four-part health check, and an Arabic RTL dashboard refreshing every 5 minutes.

With it, 10 docs: a GAQL cookbook of 19 tested recipes and 5 documented failing queries, and a forensic method for reading change_event that reveals who changed what, in Riyadh time.

Phase 0 is complete; the change guard is on the roadmap.
