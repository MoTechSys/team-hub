## LinkedIn (≤120 words)
70+ permissions, and not one extra line of code per new role.

S-ACM is an open graduation project: an Arabic academic content management system that brings students, lecturers, courses, and files into one dashboard, with dynamic permissions the administrator composes from the UI—70+ permissions across 12 sections in 3 levels.

Under the hood, a Turborepo monorepo: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, and shadcn/ui up front; Hono with PostgreSQL, Drizzle, Zod, and JWT behind. 19 pages, 14 API modules, 13 tables.

Frontend 95% complete; backend and AI features in progress. The repository is open—ask me about any part.
