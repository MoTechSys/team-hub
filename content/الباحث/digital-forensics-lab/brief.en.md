# Digital Forensics Practical Lab — an educational hands-on project

**Segment:** students
**Problem:** Digital forensics is taught from slides; students never extract an artifact themselves and prove it was not altered.
**Solution:** A lab covering all five topics (Registry, Event Log, Autopsy, FTK Imager, Browser Forensics) with real evidence: a Python parser for Chrome history that works on a copy and re-verifies hashes, a one-line PowerShell runner building a unified timeline with SHA-256 chain of custody, Sigma rules, and an HTML report tagged with MITRE ATT&CK.
**Results in numbers:** 5 topics · 19 browser records, integrity PASSED · 15 failed logons detected · 7 USB devices tied to serials · 3 Sigma rules · 16 MITRE techniques.
**Who benefits:** cybersecurity and DFIR students.
**Call to action:** The repo is public — ask about the methodology or reproduce it yourself.
