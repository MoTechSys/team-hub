## LinkedIn (≤120 words)
Nexus Notify — one notification and conversation hub for every system you run.

Each internal system sends its own messages; customers, conversations, and device fleets live in five places with no single source of truth.

I built a multi-tenant, multi-channel platform: a NestJS backend with PostgreSQL 17, Prisma, and Redis/BullMQ; an Angular 22 RTL dashboard; and a Kotlin Android app that turns dual-SIM phones into an SMS channel. Any external system integrates through one API; every conversation is stored centrally.

Result: 163 backend tests and 12/12 end-to-end, a 7-page dashboard, and an Android channel with 11 tests — all on green CI at 85% coverage.

## Instagram (≤150 words)
Customer data in five places?
When every system sends its own messages — billing from one phone, collections from another — the conversation is lost and numbers get duplicated.
Nexus Notify is one platform: a system posts a notification through one API, the message is routed by the customer's carrier to an Android phone in the branch, and the reply lands in the same conversation on a live RTL dashboard.
NestJS backend with PostgreSQL 17, Angular 22 dashboard, dual-SIM Kotlin app. 163 tests · 12/12 end-to-end · 7 pages · 85% coverage.
For companies with several systems that must reach customers from one place. Link in bio.
