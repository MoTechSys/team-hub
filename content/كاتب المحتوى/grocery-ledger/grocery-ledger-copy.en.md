## Instagram (≤150 words)
A paper notebook for debts, the shift closed in your head, and a connection that drops. That is how most corner shops run.

Grocery Ledger puts the whole ledger on the phone and works fully offline: scan barcodes with the camera or a scanner, sell by carton, bag, or piece, record receipt and payment vouchers with the amount in Arabic words, and close the shift with a Z report. Every day, an automatic backup.

The rule: nothing is ever deleted from the ledgers—it is reversed with an entry.

Version 2.2.1 · 157 passing tests · 13.3 MB · PDF and Excel export from every screen.

For groceries and small shops on one device. Link in bio.
