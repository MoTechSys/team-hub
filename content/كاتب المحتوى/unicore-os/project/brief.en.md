# UniCore-OS — an Arabic university management system with fine-grained permissions and optional AI features

**Segment:** Students (capstone projects / university systems)
**Problem:** University administration is scattered across users and roles, academic structure, quizzes and grades, and most systems are neither Arabic nor granular about permissions.
**Solution:** One system on Next.js and Prisma: 52 permissions with custom roles, a colleges → departments → majors → courses → sections hierarchy, a quiz engine (multiple choice, true/false, short answer, timer) with AI question generation and grading, files, notifications, reports and gradebook, and a trash bin so nothing is ever hard-deleted. Fully Arabic RTL interface.
**Results in numbers:** 52 permissions, 22 database models, 26 pages, 15 feature modules, zero TypeScript errors, and a 102-page crawl across 3 roles with zero issues. Release 2.0.0-rc.4.
**Who it helps:** Capstone students and computer science departments that want an open-source, extensible Arabic university system.
