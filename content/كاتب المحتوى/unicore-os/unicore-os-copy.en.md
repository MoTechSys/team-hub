# Post copy—UniCore-OS (English)

## LinkedIn (≤120 words)
University administration is scattered across systems that don't speak Arabic: users and roles here, academic structure there, quizzes and grades somewhere else.

UniCore-OS brings them into one system on Next.js and Prisma: 52 permissions with custom roles, a colleges → departments → majors → courses → sections hierarchy, a quiz engine (multiple choice, true/false, short answer, timer) with optional AI question generation and grading, files, notifications and reports, and a trash bin so nothing is ever hard-deleted. Fully Arabic RTL.

52 permissions · 22 models · 26 pages · zero TypeScript errors · release 2.0.0-rc.4.

For capstone students and CS departments. The repository is open—ask me.

## Instagram (≤150 words)
How many systems does a university need to manage its users, colleges, quizzes and grades? Usually more than one, and few of them speak Arabic.

UniCore-OS is one system: 52 fine-grained permissions that separate admin, instructor and student at the level of every action, an academic hierarchy from college to section, and a quiz engine with three question types and a timer—and, with an API key, generating questions and proposing grades with AI.

Deletion? Always to the trash bin; nothing is ever erased.

Next.js 16 · React 19 · Prisma 5 · Tailwind 4 · fully Arabic RTL.

For capstone students and CS departments. Link in bio.
