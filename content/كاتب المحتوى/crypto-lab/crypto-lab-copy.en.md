# Post copy—Crypto Lab (English)

## LinkedIn (≤120 words)
A security student sees the encrypted output but not how it got there, so the algorithm is memorized instead of understood.

Crypto Lab is a Flutter Android app that shows Caesar, Vigenère, RSA, and AES-256-GCM visually: the shift table, key extension, generating n, e, and d, and PBKDF2 derivation with nonce and GCM tag. Email OTP accounts, a SQLite lab notebook, PIN or fingerprint lock, and encrypted export—all with no server.

Signed release 1.2.0 · 54 passing tests · zero analyzer issues · ~9 MB APK · full Arabic RTL.

For cybersecurity students and capstone teams. The repository is open—ask me.

The rest is a real lab: email OTP accounts, a notebook of experiments kept on your device, PIN or fingerprint lock, and encrypted export. No server.
